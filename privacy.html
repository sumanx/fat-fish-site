Fat Fish — last updated 9 November 2026.

1. Summary

Fat Fish is a mobile client for DeepSeek Harness (DSH), a service you host yourself. We do not operate any server for this app and we do not receive your data. The app talks only to the Host you configure.

2. Data we collect

None. The app has no account system, no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no crash-reporting SDK. There is no telemetry, no usage statistics, and no device identifier collection.

3. Data stored on your device

The following is stored only in the app’s private storage on your device:

  • Host names, addresses, and the connection type you chose;
  • Access tokens, SSH passwords, private keys, and passphrases you entered;
  • Trusted certificate and SSH host-key fingerprints you confirmed;
  • Drafts, navigation state, and appearance settings.

None of this is uploaded to us or to any third party. Deleting a Host in the app also removes its saved credentials.

4. Network connections

The app connects only to the address you configure, using the credentials you enter:

  • directly over HTTPS;
  • through an SSH tunnel that you authorise on a machine you control;
  • or through a relay server you configure.

We operate no servers of our own, so no copy of your traffic reaches us. Content you read or send belongs to your Host.

5. Diagnostics

Error messages shown in the app originate from your Host or from the local connection. Nothing is reported automatically. The app can copy a redacted connection diagnostic to the clipboard, but only when you ask it to; sending that text anywhere is your own action.

6. Permissions

The app requests local network access in order to reach a Host on your network. It does not request access to your photo library, contacts, location, microphone, or camera.

7. Children

Fat Fish is a developer tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

8. Your choices

You can remove all locally stored data by deleting individual Hosts in the app or by deleting the app. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new date.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: github.com/sumanx/fat-fish-site/issues.

Fat Fish is an independent client and is not affiliated with the vendor of DeepSeek Harness.
